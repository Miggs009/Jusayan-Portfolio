    Research Papers

    SPORTS MATCH PREDICTION ANALYSIS APPLYING MACHINE LEARNING MODEL WITH RULE-BASED APPROACH

    This research introduces an innovative approach to sports match prediction, combining machine learning model with a rule-based approach, focusing on the Philippine Basketball Association (PBA) and Premier Volleyball League (PVL). Objectives include creating a hybrid model, optimizing datasets, and deploying the model in a web application. The research shows promising accuracy for basketball (82.86%) and volleyball (90.20%), with random forest model and feature importance analysis highlighting key predictors. The conclusion emphasizes the model's value in sports prediction and recommends further exploration of the proposed approach.

    EVALUATION OF ETHANOL PROPERTY IN GRAPE SEAWEED (CAULERPA RACEMOSA) IN POTENTIAL BIOETHANOL PRODUCTION

    High cost of fuels is emerging in Philippine market. Aside from this, many rural areas in Philippines are not capable to avail or to have access in utilizing fuels. This study was conducted to identify the total ethanolic percentage of Grape Seaweed. In that way, evaluation of the potential of Grape seaweed as an alternative fuel is suggested. Through the tests, comparison between the ethanolic property of Caulerpa racemosa and the ethanol that is commercially consumed was done. The mean concentration of ethanol from Grape seaweed was obtained using gas cromatograph and measured 3.47 %. The Commercially-available biofuel from the study of Sheik et.al 2016 measured 2. 325 % and the difference between the two were computed as 1.131%. This implies that the ethanol from Grape seaweed obtained a higher concentration of ethanol than commercial biofuels and is a possible source for bioethanol extraction using brewer’s yeast as fermentation agent.
